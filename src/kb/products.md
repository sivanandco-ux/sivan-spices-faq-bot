# Sivan Spices — Product Catalog

All products: Made in a home kitchen in Fremont, California, under a California Class B Cottage Food Operation permit (issued by Alameda County). Vegan. No preservatives, no artificial colors or flavors. No garlic, no onion. Net weight 100 grams / 3.5 oz per sachet.

**Facility allergen warning (applies to every product below): Facility handles nuts and gluten.**
This is a facility cross-contact warning, separate from each product's own ingredient list below. A product can be gluten-free as formulated and still carry this warning because it is made in a kitchen that also processes nut- and gluten-containing ingredients for other products.

## Garam Masala
Category: Premium All-Purpose Aromatic.
Ingredients: Coriander seeds, Cumin seeds, Turmeric, Black pepper, Black Cardamom, Green Cardamom, Dried red chilies, Fennel seeds, Bay leaf, Cloves, Mace, Cinnamon, Star Anise.
No direct nut, sesame, dairy, soy, egg, fish, or shellfish ingredients.
Usage: Seasoning for rice, quinoa, soups, curries, chicken, meats, and seafood.

## Sambar & Rasam Powder
Category: Aromatic Broth Base.
Ingredients: Coriander seeds, Cumin seeds, Turmeric, Split chickpeas (Chana dal), Split pigeon peas (Toor dal), Black pepper, Fenugreek seeds, Mustard seeds, Dried red chilies, Sesame oil.
Contains sesame (sesame oil) as a direct ingredient. Contains chickpeas (a legume).
Usage: 10-minute base for tomato soups, lentil consommés, or stews. Pairs with rice, quinoa, idli, dosa, upma.

## Cardamom GrainMeal
Category: Ancient Grain Powerhouse.
Ingredients: Black rice, Black urad, Navara rice, Kullakar rice, Fox millet, Pearl millet, Kodo millet, Dry ginger, Cinnamon, Cardamom, Nutmeg, Mace.
No direct nut, sesame, dairy, soy, egg, fish, or shellfish ingredients.
Usage: 2-minute warm porridge or chilled wellness drink. Cook in the microwave or on the stovetop.

## Dosa Gun Powder ("Dosai" Seasoning)
Category: Ready to Eat Mix.
Ingredients: Whole black gram (Udad dal), Dried red chilies, Asafoetida, Sesame oil, Salt.
Contains sesame (sesame oil) as a direct ingredient.
Usage: Pair with idli and dosa. Topper for avocado toast, eggs, or roasted chickpeas.

## Lentil Mix
Category: Ready to Eat Mix / Savory Protein Topper.
Ingredients: Split Pigeon Peas (Toor dal), Dried red chilies, Black pepper, Cumin, Asafoetida, Dried curry leaves, Salt.
No direct nut, sesame, dairy, soy, egg, fish, or shellfish ingredients. Contains pigeon peas (a legume).
Usage: Instant flavor for rice, quinoa, noodles, and roasted vegetables. Forms a savory crust when roasting, toasting, or air-frying vegetables and proteins.

## Curry Leaf Mix
Category: Ready to Eat Mix / Savory Protein Topper.
Ingredients: Fresh Curry leaves, Turmeric, Split chickpeas (Chana dal), Black gram (Urad dal), Dried red chilies, Asafoetida, Salt, Ginger, Sesame oil.
Contains sesame (sesame oil) as a direct ingredient. Contains chickpeas (a legume).
Usage: Instant flavor for rice, quinoa, noodles, and roasted vegetables. Forms a savory crust when roasting, toasting, or air-frying vegetables and proteins.

## Sesame Mix
Category: Ready to Eat Mix / Savory Protein Topper.
Ingredients: Whole black gram (Udad dal), Pigeon Peas (Toor dal), Dried red chilies, Sesame seeds, Black pepper, Jeera (cumin), Asafoetida, Salt, Sesame oil.
Contains sesame (sesame seeds AND sesame oil) as a direct ingredient — this is the most sesame-forward product in the line. Not appropriate for anyone with a sesame allergy.
Usage: Instant flavor for rice, quinoa, noodles, and roasted vegetables. Forms a savory crust when roasting, toasting, or air-frying vegetables and proteins.

## Coconut Mix
Category: Ready to Eat Mix.
Ingredients: Whole black gram (Udad dal), Fresh coconut, Dried red chilies, Sesame seeds, Asafoetida, Dried curry leaves, Salt.
Contains sesame (sesame seeds) as a direct ingredient. Contains coconut — the FDA does not classify coconut as a tree nut, but some individuals with tree nut allergies do react to coconut; flag this explicitly if asked and recommend checking with a doctor rather than asserting it is safe.
Usage: Instant flavor for rice, quinoa, noodles, and roasted vegetables. Forms a savory crust when roasting, toasting, or air-frying vegetables and proteins.
