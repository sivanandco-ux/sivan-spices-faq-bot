# Sivan Spices — Company Information & Policies

## About
Sivan Spices is an artisanal Indian spice mix brand based in Fremont, California. All products are made in a home kitchen under a California Class B Cottage Food Operation (CFO) permit, issued by Alameda County. The brand was founded by Tara Jagannathan, a technologist and product leader with over 30 years of experience (including at Cisco and Zoom), who applied her product background to recreating authentic, traditional South Indian family recipes as small-batch, slow-roasted, preservative-free spice blends.

Mission: To offer an alternative to mass-produced spice mixes with artificial fillers, by providing clean-label, small-batch blends that let busy families recreate authentic home-cooked meals in minutes.

## Where we sell
Sivan Spices ships anywhere within the United States. We do not currently ship internationally or to U.S. territories outside the 50 states — if asked about international shipping, say it is not currently supported and suggest contacting contact@sivanspices.com to check for updates.

## Shipping
Orders are placed through our Shopify store at www.sivanspices.com. At checkout, the customer selects their preferred shipping speed/carrier from the options Shopify presents — Sivan Spices does not enforce one fixed shipping method or rate; the cost and estimated delivery time depend on the option the customer selects at checkout.

## Order processing time
As a small-batch, made-to-order cottage food business, please allow 1–3 business days for order processing before a package ships, in addition to the shipping transit time selected at checkout. For an exact status on a specific order, contact contact@sivanspices.com with the order number.

## Returns & refunds
Because these are consumable food products made in small batches, we are not able to accept returns of opened products. If an order arrives damaged, incorrect, or you are otherwise unsatisfied with the quality of a sealed product, contact contact@sivanspices.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number and we will work with you on a replacement or refund on a case-by-case basis.

## Shelf life & storage
Store sachets in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and reseal tightly after opening to preserve freshness, since these blends contain no preservatives. For a specific best-by date, refer to the date printed on your sachet, or contact contact@sivanspices.com.

## Recipes
If a customer asks about recipes or how to use a product in cooking, direct them to our Kitchen Helper recipe bot at https://sivan-co-2.myshopify.com/pages/kitchen-helper — it can suggest recipes and cooking ideas using Sivan Spices products.

## What we do not make claims about
Sivan Spices products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Any wellness language used in product descriptions (e.g., "antioxidant-rich," "supports immune health") describes the known properties of traditional ingredients and is not a medical claim about the packaged product. If a customer asks a medical or health question, encourage them to consult a healthcare provider rather than answering as medical advice.
